Ultralytics HUB Quickstart
Ultralytics HUB
is designed to be user-friendly and intuitive, allowing users to quickly upload their datasets and train new YOLO models. It also offers a range of pre-trained models to choose from, making it extremely easy for users to get started. Once a model is trained, it can be effortlessly previewed in the
Ultralytics HUB App
before being deployed for real-time classification,
object detection
, and
instance segmentation
tasks.

How to train Ultralytics YOLO11 on Custom Dataset using Ultralytics HUB | HUB Datasets 🚀
Get Started
Ultralytics HUB
offers a variety of easy signup options. You can register and log in using your Google, Apple, or GitHub accounts, or simply with your email address.
During the signup, you will be asked to complete your profile.
Tip
You can update your profile from the
Account
tab on the
Settings
page.
Home
After signing in, you will be directed to the
Home
page of
Ultralytics HUB
, which provides a comprehensive overview, quick links, and updates.
The sidebar conveniently offers links to important modules of the platform, such as
Datasets
,
Projects
, and
Models
.
Recent
You can easily search globally or directly access your last updated
Datasets
,
Projects
, or
Models
using the Recent card on the
Home
page.
Upload Dataset
You can upload a dataset directly from the
Home
page. Ultralytics HUB supports various dataset formats and makes it easy to prepare your data for
training custom YOLO models
.
Read more about
datasets
and how to prepare them for optimal training results.
Create Project
You can create a project directly from the
Home
page. Projects help you organize related models and experiments in one place, making it easier to track progress and compare results.
Read more about
projects
and how they can streamline your workflow.
Train Model
You can train a model directly from the
Home
page. Ultralytics HUB offers multiple training options including
cloud training
, Google Colab integration, or using your own hardware.
Read more about
models
and the various architectures available for your computer vision tasks.
Feedback
We value your feedback! Feel free to leave a review at any time to help us improve the platform.
Info
Only our team will see your feedback, and we will use it to improve our platform.
Need Help?
If you encounter any issues or have questions, we're here to assist you.
You can report a bug, request a feature, or ask a question on
GitHub
.
Note
When reporting a bug, please include your Environment Details from the
Support
page.
Tip
You can join our
Discord
community for questions and discussions!
